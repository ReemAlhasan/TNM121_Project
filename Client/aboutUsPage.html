How to play

Start the game from the home page.

You will see two movies: the current one and the next one.

Guess if the next movie has a higher or lower IMDB rating than the current one.

Click "Higher" or "Lower".

If correct, your score increases and you continue; if wrong, the game ends.

Aim for the highest score possible!

Game information

Higher Lower Game is a quiz game where you have to guess if the next movie has higher or lower rating than the previous one. The game is based on the IMDB ratings of movies, and it is a fun way to test your knowledge of movies and their ratings.

Before each game you may customize the experience through different settings, allowing you to tailor the game to your preferences and adjust the difficulty level.
